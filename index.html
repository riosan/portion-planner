Portion Planner — User Guide

Welcome to the Portion Planner! This application is designed to automate and simplify your shift calculations, dough batch scheduling, and recipe scaling directly on the bakery floor.

🔐 1. Getting Started & Syncing Data

  • Google Sign-In: At the top of the screen, you will see a "Sign in with Google" button. Clicking this allows you to automatically back up and sync your custom presets and calculation history to the cloud.
  • Local Mode: If you choose not to log in, the app works entirely offline and saves your progress locally in your browser.
  • Language Toggle: Switch between English (EN) and Dutch (NL) instantly using the buttons in the top header. All numbers, weights, and dates will format automatically according to the selected country's standards.

⏱️ 2. Setting Up Your Shift

  • Production Window: Enter your shift's starting time in "Production start" and finishing time in "Production end".
  • Tip: If you are starting right now, simply tap the "Now" button to grab your current phone/computer time instantly.
  • Portion Time: In "Full portion time, min", type how many minutes it takes to complete a single standard portion cycle.
  • Portion Weight: In "Full portion weight, kg", enter the total target weight of one full batch.
  • Current Portion Number: If you are running calculations from the very beginning of the shift, leave this at 1. If you are recalculating a schedule mid-shift, change this to the number of the batch you are currently mixing.

🔄 3. Using Shift Presets

To save time, the application comes with standard pre-configured shifts:

  • Click the "Presets" dropdown menu to select a shift (e.g., Standard, Morning shift, Evening shift).
  • Save Custom Presets: If you adjust the times and want to remember them for later, click "Save preset", type a name, and it will be saved to your account.
  • Delete Presets: Custom presets can be removed by selecting them and clicking "Delete preset". (Built-in standard presets cannot be deleted).

⚖️ 4. Scaling Additives (Fat, Water, Sugar, Flour)

If your current recipe requires adjustments per portion, use the "Additives per full portion" block.

  • Enter the values in kilograms or liters for a full portion.
  • Negative Values: The app fully supports negative values (e.g., -1.5 if you need to subtract water or flour due to dough consistency). On mobile devices, tap the symbols panel on your keyboard to type the minus (-) sign.
  • The system will automatically calculate the exact proportion of these additives for every batch, including the final partial batch.
  • Click "Reset additives" to quickly clear all additive inputs back to empty.

☕ 5. Planning Breaks & Technical Pauses

The scheduler allows you to pause production without messing up your end-time math:

  • Tap "Add break".
  • Give the break a name (e.g., «Lunch», «Knife Cleaning», «Technical Pause»).
  • Enter the Break start and Break end times.
  • The calculator will automatically freeze any active portion timeline during this window and smoothly resume it the exact minute the break ends.
  • To remove a break, tap the "X" button next to it.

📊 6. Reading Results & Sharing

Once all data is filled out, tap the large green "Calculate" button. The app generates a timeline dashboard:

  • Summary Box: Shows total counts of expected portions, accumulated shift weights, and the combined sum of all required additives.
  • Night Shift Indicator: If your shift crosses midnight, subsequent times will safely display a +1 badge next to the hour.
  • Incomplete Last Portion: If your final batch doesn't have enough time to finish completely before "Production end", the app automatically calculates its reduced weight and scaled ingredients so you don't waste raw materials.
  • Smart Mobile View: If your shift contains more than 8 portions, the middle rows of the table will collapse automatically so you don't have to scroll endlessly on a mobile phone screen.
  • Sharing the Plan: Tap "Copy schedule". A cleanly formatted text timeline will be copied to your clipboard. You can instantly paste it directly into WhatsApp, Teams, or any work chat to share it with your team.
